# Mathematical Modeling Workflow Policy

This plugin coordinates an evidence-first mathematical-modeling workflow. The user's request and the current contest rules remain authoritative.

## Core invariants

- Keep raw data unchanged. Record every cleaning, filtering, aggregation, and unit conversion rule.
- Treat each subquestion as a gated unit with one canonical manifest under `planning/manifests/`.
- Autonomous end-to-end execution and human review boundary: by default, run in autonomous mode where the AI drives the workflow end-to-end without requiring intermediate human intervention or cognitive burden. When no human preference is explicitly provided, the AI autonomously selects the optimal method following Occam's razor (simple model first), executes experiments, judges and freezes results, and drafts paper sections. The decision ledger records these as autonomous decisions (`decided_by: "autonomous_agent"` citing concrete evidence), enabling gates G1 through G5.6 to pass automatically. The human serves as the final authority who reviews and audits the completed master paper and rendered submission artifacts.
- Authentic and authoritative references: every cited reference must be 100% genuine, verifiable, and authoritative. Prioritize classic modeling/statistical textbooks (e.g., Jiang Qiyuan, Si Shoukui, Li Hang, Zhou Zhihua), foundational peer-reviewed papers (e.g., Saaty AHP, Hwang & Yoon TOPSIS, Breiman Random Forests), national/industry standards, or official statistical yearbooks. Absolutely no fabricated authors, papers, venues, or DOIs. In-text citations must match the bibliography 1-to-1 following the GB/T 7714-2015 standard; do not pad bibliographies with unreferenced or non-authoritative entries.
- Do not invent data, results, citations, figures, robustness evidence, or interpretations.
- Every numerical paper claim must trace to a canonical result artifact from the approved run.
- Separate exploratory artifacts from frozen submission artifacts.
- A formal statistical figure must be generated deterministically from saved evidence. Generative image tools may be used only for non-evidentiary illustrations.
- Model selection and Occam's razor: permit and encourage web research for problem background, domain mechanisms, and prior knowledge to ground assumptions and modeling. When both a complex model and a simple model can solve the problem, strictly prioritize the simpler model (favoring interpretability, numerical stability, and robustness over gratuitous black-box complexity). Prefer a main method, a usable baseline, and at most one trigger-based fallback. Do not add models merely for breadth.
- Enforce non-standard code variable and file naming: keep both variable names and generated file names (scripts, intermediate data, figures, tables) simple and readable, using simple vocabulary (e.g., basic English words like `clean`, `data`, `main`, `test`, `model`, `fig`, `tab`, `q1`) or full Pinyin for domain/business concepts (e.g., `fenghua`, `wenshi`, `qianbei`), with exact chemical formulas directly used for chemical substances (e.g., `Pb`, `SiO2`). Avoid excessively long, complex, or obscure English filenames. Third-party library keyword arguments/parameters and standard mathematical abbreviations (e.g., `opt`, `loss`, `res`, `grad`, `iter`, `dim`, `dist`, `pred`, `step`) are exempted from Pinyin enforcement.
- Enforce paper writing and language style: highlight specialized domain and statistical technical terms using bold (`**term**`) to facilitate authors rephrasing them into conversational language. Do not invent unnatural, terse compressed phrases; explain concepts fully. Permit and encourage natural, intuitive, and conversational explanations where helpful for conceptual clarity. When the contest problem statement does not explicitly divide a question into subquestions, do not write mechanical phrases like "the first subquestion of question one"; omit structural subjects and transition naturally using concrete task objectives.
- Model assumptions and conventions: every paper must include explicit assumptions; title the section "模型假设与约定" if conventions are present, or "模型假设" if conventions are absent. Format each assumption statement using the standard template "假设……" (e.g., `假设1：假设……`).
- Mandatory in-text figure discussion: every figure placed in the paper must be followed immediately by in-depth discussion centered on the visual evidence, explicitly deriving findings and conclusions (e.g., "通过上述图片得出……", "由图X可以看出……") rather than leaving visuals unanalyzed.
- Unified scientific chart color scheme: all generated charts (Python/MATLAB) must strictly adhere to the unified scientific color system defined in `.agents/rules/scientific-chart-color-scheme.md` and `math-figure-generator/references/color-systems.md` (Extreme high `#6A0624`, high `#B80824`, medium-high `#D71C2C`, lower-high `#FEAB88`, neutral-light `#FDD2BC`, neutral-low `#C7E0ED`, medium-low `#6FAFD2`, low `#327DB7`, lower `#134B87`, extreme-low `#053061`). Correlation heatmaps use the blue-to-red diverging colormap centered at zero; sequential metrics use monotonic blue or red palettes; line/bar charts adopt fixed categorical colors (`#134B87`, `#D71C2C`, `#327DB7`, `#FEAB88`, `#6A0624`, `#6FAFD2`), and baseline curves use grey `#767676` dashed lines, ensuring flawless aesthetic harmony across all paper figures.
- National contest template and paper structure contract: final paper assembly and formatting must strictly follow the official national contest standard template (`assets/templates/national_contest_template.docx`, sourced from user's `国赛模板 (1).docx`) and `.agents/rules/national-contest-paper-structure.md`. This includes the standard twelve-part structure: five-part abstract (overview, per-question quantitative/methodology conclusions, highlights/generalization), non-plagiarized problem restatement, problem analysis without premature results, model assumptions and conventions, standard three-line symbol table, independent data preprocessing, model formulation and solution, model evaluation with advantages and improvements, mandatory official AI tool usage declaration, GB/T 7714-2015 authentic references, and Appendix A supporting material file list. For dual-track Word/LaTeX rendering: Word output must have zero raw LaTeX syntax残留 and use clean Unicode/subscripts; LaTeX papers must use `tabularx` + `booktabs` with adaptive `X` columns (or `\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{...}`) for all three-line tables to guarantee that tables fit within `\textwidth` without right-side clipping.
- Figure and table numbering lifecycle: initially number figures and tables by chapter (e.g., `图1-1`, `表1-1`) during section drafting and initial assembly. After human review confirms the master paper without error, sequentially renumber all figures and tables globally (e.g., `图1, 图2...` and `表1, 表2...`) and synchronously update all in-text cross-references using the automated script `scripts/renumber_figures_tables.py` before final submission.
- A semantic change to frozen evidence requires affected work to be rerun and re-frozen before paper claims are updated.
- Gate state is enforced from on-disk evidence by `scripts/gate_check.py`, not inferred from a self-reported manifest. Run its `guard` action before model-code generation, freezing, paper writing, and final assembly; a non-zero exit blocks the action. A manifest that runs ahead of evidence is an integrity violation.

## Rigor profiles

- `lean`: optimize for rapid, reviewable modeling. Keep manifests, method cards, human decisions, risk probes, run summaries, and compact evidence.
- `submission`: additionally require final explanations, robustness, a signed solution package, frozen numbers, formal figure plans, paper sections, full-paper assembly, render verification, and three independent final audits.

## Final submission boundary

Do not mark the submission complete until every active subquestion passes G5, the master paper passes G5.5, the rendered DOCX/PDF passes G5.6, and consistency, completeness, and quality-assurance audits pass G6.

Never place project-specific progress items in this packaged policy. Current gaps belong in project manifests or a generated status report.
